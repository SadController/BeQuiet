# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - 2026-10-11

- Choose favorites in the main window instead of setting priorities.
- Left-click or right-click the tray icon to open the favorites menu.
- Select one or more apps to focus on using the tray menu checkboxes.
- Selecting an app in the tray immediately turns down other apps; clearing the last selection restores their volume.
- Favorites and focus selections are saved. Closed apps remain in favorites.
- Saved priorities from the previous version migrate to favorites and focus selections.
- Original volume levels are tracked separately for each audio session.
- The interface, tray menu, tooltips, status messages, documentation, and project files are all in English.
- Tested on Windows 10 Enterprise LTSC 2021 (19044), x64.
